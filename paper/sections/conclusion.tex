\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method shows that pretrained modulation pathways are a useful substrate for
efficient test-time adaptation of large video diffusion transformers. By
sharing a single \dimt-dimensional residual across all \nblocks blocks and
letting the frozen adaLN projections supply the per-block de-tying, \method
adapts denoising behavior with a tiny trainable surface, no new layers, and
no source-data access at adaptation time. The broader lesson is that
behavior-level adaptation through pretrained conditioning interfaces is
preferable to adding large free adapter subspaces when the adaptation signal
is itself small. Pushing the same interface to long-horizon video generation
is the most natural next direction.
